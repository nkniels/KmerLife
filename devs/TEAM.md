## How to claim a slot

1. Edit the row for the dev number you are taking.
2. Fill in **Claimed by** (your name or handle), **Contact** (GitHub username, email, or Telegram), and **Date claimed**.
3. Change **Status** to 🔴 Taken.
4. Open a PR — DEV-01 reviews it.

## Status legend

| Icon | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 🟢 Open | Nobody has claimed this slot yet |
| 🔴 Taken | Actively owned by someone |
| 🟡 Paused | Owner is temporarily unavailable; slot may be handed off |

## Notes

- One person, one slot. If the team grows beyond six, raise it with DEV-01 first.
- AI agent sessions working a slot must still follow the ownership rules in `devs/DEV-0X.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
- If you hand off a slot, update both this file and notify the team.
